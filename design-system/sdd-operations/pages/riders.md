# Rider Directory

Follow the shared MASTER.md palette, IBM Plex Sans and light/dark tokens.

- Header: directory identity, source actions, last loaded count. Management actions retain existing permissions.
- Four summary cards: total, active, current shift and missing delivery geography.
- Five area cards: total roster, share of KV1–KV5, active/inactive split. Selecting an area clears other filters and filters the directory; selecting it again restores all areas.
- Detail panels: top five delivery districts within the selected area and COT composition. Clicking a district filters both area and district.
- Analysis comes from the complete cached registry before pagination. It stays independent of search, status and table-page filters; the selected area changes the detail panels.
- COT 1 includes 1.1/1.2. Unknown COT and missing district remain explicit. Active status is a registry status, not attendance.
- Preserve profile drill-down, import, export template, Plan sync, scope-limited editing and recruitment section.
- Five area columns on wide screens, three on medium screens, two on mobile. Tables scroll inside their own frame. Every chart has visible values and keyboard-operable controls.
